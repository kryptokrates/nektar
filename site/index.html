Honest by Design

What newbies ask first

Do I need BSV or a wallet to try it?

No. Testnet comes with built-in free test sats in 1 click. Real BSV Mainnet is strictly opt-in and protected by fail-closed SpendGuard caps.

Are my private memories shared?

Never. Everything stays encrypted in your local SQLite vault (BRC-369). Your agent only quotes shards you explicitly choose to list for sale.

What does a receipt prove?

Cryptographic proofs establish that a claim was recorded, unaltered, and derivable on BSV — never that the text is objectively true.
