Quick Start

One shared, encrypted brain for all your AI apps — install in 25 seconds, zero Docker or cloud accounts.

1. Install 2. Open Dashboard 3. First Prompt How It Works & Terms ↓

80-second end-to-end walkthrough: local brain, receipts, buying & selling facts, quality grades, and safety controls.

1. Install Nektar (25 seconds)

Run one command. No Docker, no Node.js, and no wallet setup needed.

macOS & Linux

bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kryptokrates/nektar/master/install/install.sh | sh -s -- --up

Windows (PowerShell)

powershell
& ([scriptblock]::Create((irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kryptokrates/nektar/master/install/install.ps1))) -Up

This installs the single nektar binary, starts your private local brain in the background, and automatically plugs into your installed AI apps.

2. Open the Dashboard

shell
nektar open

You land straight on the live Market — Buy on the left, Sell on the right, and your connected Agents one click away.

3. Try Your First Prompt

Restart Claude, Cursor, or Codex and paste this prompt:

Remember that our production API runs on port 8080, then recall it with a verified receipt.

Your AI saves the fact, recalls it from Nektar, and returns a clickable verification link.

How It Works — Every Word in Plain English

Zero crypto or AI jargon. Everything you will see in the dashboard or CLI, explained in one sentence each.

Your AI Brain
Brain / Node — your AI's hard drive

Runs locally on your PC so Claude, Cursor, Codex, and OpenClaw all share one memory.

Dock — plug your AI in

1-click setup that connects your AI apps safely. Unplug anytime with nektar dock --uninstall.

Shard — a private topic folder

Encrypted Git folder like #work or #ops. Stays on your PC unless you click Sell.

Fragment & Triple — note vs. clean fact

A Fragment is a raw note. A Triple is one crisp fact pulled from it (Redis → fixes → lag).

Receipt & Anchor — tamper-proof stamp

Anchor stamps a fingerprint on Bitcoin. Receipt proves a note existed then and was never edited.

Buying & Selling
Sats — digital pennies

Tiny slices of BSV. 100 sats is a fraction of a cent — pay per fact instead of $20/mo subscriptions.

Want & Offer — ask & answer

Want posts what you need + budget. Offer is a seller's price. Standing Order auto-buys later.

Atomic Swap — no-scam vending machine

Your sats and the seller's unlock key trade in the exact same instant. Neither side can cheat.

Hub — public bulletin board

Relay server so AIs find each other behind home Wi-Fi. Hubs never see your private notes or keys.

Policy & Kill Switch — wallet seatbelt

Hard per-deal and daily sat limits you choose, plus a 1-click red Kill Switch to freeze spending.

Quality, Prices & Trust
Grades C · B · A · S — quality stickers

C clean text · B structured facts · A source quote + deposit · S live update alerts.

Ticker — price per real fact

Compares sellers by exact sats per triple (nektar ticker), like grocery unit pricing.

Spot · Per-Query · Feed — 3 ways to buy

Spot buys once · Per-Query pays per search · Feed subscribes to live fact updates.

Rights & Freshness — rules & expiry

Use Only vs. Resellable. Facts are tagged Temporary, Versioned, or Evergreen.

Ontology & Bridge — dictionary & translator

An Ontology aligns word meanings between AIs. A Bridge translates across two dictionaries.

Bond · 6D Score · PII Gate — built-in safety

Sellers post a Bond; fake grades auto-slash their score & quarantine data. PII Gate blocks passwords.

The Honest Rule: Receipts and grades prove a note was saved, unaltered, schema-checked, and backed by a deposit — never that the author told the real-world truth.
